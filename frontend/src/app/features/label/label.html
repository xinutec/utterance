Mark the syllables

Mark every syllable in one unbroken stretch — ten seconds is plenty. Tap M as it plays, then put the marks right on the chart.

Play the take and tap once per syllable — the button does the same.

Don't aim for precision while tapping. A tap always lands a little after the sound; put it right afterwards on the chart.

On the chart: drag a line to move it, click empty space to add one, click a line to remove it. Zoom in to place them closely.

A complete stretch beats a long one. Inside a stretch where nothing is missed, anything the analyser finds that you did not mark is a mistake it made — which is the whole measurement. A gap in the middle looks exactly like one of those mistakes, so if you tire, stop and say where.

Slow it to 0.25× to hear where a consonant begins.

Everything saves itself.
