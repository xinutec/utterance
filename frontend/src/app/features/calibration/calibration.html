Seven short recordings, one at a time. Do them in a quiet room, a hand's width from the microphone, slightly off to one side so plosives don't punch it — and keep that same position throughout, because several of these get compared to each other.

Nothing is being levelled or noise-reduced: automatic gain control and noise suppression are switched off on purpose, so what you do is what gets measured. No need to compensate for anything.
